# 🏘️ Pixel Town - Strategy Game

A fun pixel-based town management strategy game built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, and Canvas. Run and build your own thriving town!

## 🎮 Game Overview

Pixel Town is a strategic resource management game where you build and manage a pixel-based town. Construct various buildings to generate resources, manage your population, and expand your town over time.

## 📋 Features

- **Building System**: 5 different building types with unique production values
  - 🏠 **House**: Generate population and small gold
  - 🌾 **Farm**: Produce food and population
  - 🏪 **Market**: Generate gold and food
  - ⛏️ **Mine**: High gold production
  - 🏰 **Barracks**: Population and defense support

- **Resource Management**:
  - 💰 **Gold**: Currency for building and trading
  - 👥 **Population**: Generated by houses and farms
  - 🌾 **Food**: Consumed by population, generated by farms and markets

- **Day/Night Cycle**: Progress through days to generate resources from your buildings

- **Dynamic UI**: Real-time resource tracking and building status

- **Grid-based Placement**: Easy-to-use click-and-place building system

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **Start the Game**: Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. **Select a Building**: Click on any building button in the sidebar
3. **Place Buildings**: Click on the grid to place your selected building (requires gold)
4. **Manage Resources**: Monitor your resources at the top of the screen
5. **Progress Through Days**: Click "Next Day" to advance time and generate resources
6. **Expand Your Town**: Use generated resources to build more buildings
7. **Strategic Planning**: Balance different building types to maximize efficiency

## 💡 Strategy Tips

- **Early Game**: Build houses first to grow your population
- **Mid Game**: Add farms and markets for food and gold production
- **Late Game**: Focus on mines for high gold income
- **Balance**: Don't neglect food production; your population needs to eat!
- **Synergy**: Different building types complement each other

## 🛠️ Building Costs & Production

| Building | Cost | Produces | Notes |
|----------|------|----------|-------|
| House | 30 | +2 Population, +1 Gold | Foundation of your town |
| Farm | 40 | +5 Food, +1 Population | Essential for food supply |
| Market | 60 | +8 Gold, +2 Food | Great all-rounder |
| Mine | 80 | +15 Gold | High cost, high reward |
| Barracks | 100 | +3 Population, +2 Gold | Late-game building |

Production values are multiplied by building level (currently all level 1).

## 📊 Game Mechanics

- Each day, all buildings produce resources based on their type and level
- Population consumes 0.5 food per person per day
- If you run out of food, population decreases by 20%
- Random events occasionally provide bonus gold
- You can reset the game anytime to start fresh

## 🎨 Technical Stack

- **Frontend**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Rendering**: HTML5 Canvas
- **Styling**: CSS3 with gradients and animations
- **Architecture**: Object-oriented design with Game, Town, and Building classes
